# BoneLock — preliminary product and patent screen

Search date: 14 September 2026. This is a quick public-web screen, not a comprehensive prior-art search, patentability opinion, freedom-to-operate opinion, or trademark clearance.

## Result

No exact match to the approved BoneLock visual concept was identified in the sources successfully inspected. That is NOT verification that nobody uses it. Existing antenna winders, insulators, strain-relief arrangements and adjustable antenna reels are relevant precedents. The potential distinction to investigate is the particular combination of a dedicated winding waist, an isolated support neck/eye, and retained friction-wrap posts in a compact body.

## Commercial interpretation

Proceeding with an engineering prototype is reasonable; calling it verified unique is not. Do not advertise the broad idea of winding an antenna wire or combining a winder with antenna hardware as proven novel. A subsequent review should inspect the unresolved maker models, relevant patent drawings and all claims/families, related reel and line-cleat mechanisms, and design patents in intended sales jurisdictions. BoneLock remains a working name; trademark availability was not checked.

The current printable prototype also differs from the attractive rendering: its keeper uses nylon screws. Any assessment of a future captive sliding latch must use that latch's actual geometry rather than the rendering alone.
